Burning sends $FATHOOD to the burn address 0x…dEaD, where nobody can ever spend it. Fewer tokens in circulation, and you get the unlock forever.

Pick your wallet. Use one that supports Robinhood Chain (EVM), like MetaMask or Rabby. Solana-only wallets won't work.

Don't see MetaMask or Rabby? Install MetaMask or Rabby, then refresh this page. On a phone, open this page in the MetaMask app.

No EVM wallet found in this browser. Install MetaMask or Rabby, or open this page inside your wallet app's browser. Open in the MetaMask app

Burning is permanent. The tokens are destroyed and can't be refunded. Your wallet will ask you to approve the transaction. Fathood never sees your keys, and you should never type your seed phrase anywhere.
