HOW TO START

  1. Click Download and save the .zip file.
  2. Right-click the zip → Extract All... → Extract. (Don't play from inside the zip.)
  3. Open the new folder and double-click FathoodBattlegrounds.exe.
  4. Windows may show a blue box "Windows protected your PC". That's normal for small games that aren't from a big studio: click More info → Run anyway.
  5. Make your account name, pick a fighter in the Burger Balloon and pull the JUMP! lever.
  6. Everyone rides the balloon over the island: press Space to jump out, steer with W A S D, and your burger balloon opens near the ground.

The game is online only for the leaderboard: your name, K.O.s and placement are sent after each match. Nothing else is collected.

CONTROLS

W A S D move   Space jump   Shift sprint
Left click shoot / punch
Right click special ability
R reload   G grenade   T change grenade
Wheel / 123 / Q switch gun
E smash crate   F pick up gun   X drop gun
H heal   Tab bag   M big map
Ctrl + Wheel zoom   Esc show mouse
Z (hold) sniper scope
F at the battleship gangway: board & fire missiles
Space in the Burger Balloon: jump out

Every key can be changed in the game: SETTINGS in the lobby (any key or mouse button).
