How to run the $1 test

  1. Press Turn test prices ON above. The red bar appears at the top.
  2. Go to the Fat List section here, open Add a coin, paste the $FATHOOD contract address, press Find details, then Add to the Fat List.
  3. On your phone, open fathood.org/fatlist.html inside your wallet app’s browser (MetaMask, Rabby or OKX). Open the Grow tab, search “fathood” and pick it. The 10-boost pack shows $1.
  4. Pick Robinhood Chain and ETH (or USDG/USDC), connect your wallet and confirm the $1 payment in your wallet. Wait for “Paid. You’re in.”
  5. Check that $FATHOOD shows the ⚡10 boost badge on the board (it can take a minute), and that the payment is listed under Fat List → Payments, marked “TEST PRICE”.
  6. Come back here and press Turn test prices OFF. The red bar disappears and normal prices are back.
