# Fathood ideas

Things crypto is missing that Fathood can build. None of them needs a smart contract or holding anyone's money.
Saved 20 Nov 2026. Status: **building** = in progress, **idea** = not started.

## Big ideas

1. **Fat Check: a rug-pull check in plain English** (building)
   A 🟢 / 🟡 / 🔴 light on every coin page: can you sell it (honeypot test), is the liquidity tiny or unlocked,
   do a few wallets hold most of the supply, has the dev sold. Ends with a one-line verdict.

2. **Caller Scoreboard: "Who actually called it?"** (building)
   When someone posts about a coin in Hood Talk, save the price at that moment and track it after 1 day, 1 week and 1 month.
   Public leaderboard of the best and worst callers, with an accuracy badge on each profile.

3. **Holder perks in the game** (idea)
   Connect a wallet in Fathood Battlegrounds (read-only balance check). Holding $FATHOOD unlocks skins, hats or a golden flame.
   Bigger bag, bigger perks.

## More ideas

4. **Dev report card** (idea): follow dev wallets across launches and flag serial ruggers before people buy.
5. **Trade brag cards** (idea): turn a trade (win or loss) into a funny Fathood meme card to share on X.
6. **Fat Alerts on Telegram** (idea): new coins, big buys, price moves and coins you follow. Goes with the Telegram buy bot.
7. **Fat Score** (idea): one number per coin mixing votes, safety and real trading, so newcomers know where to start.
8. **Launch kit for new teams** (idea): Torchpad launch checklist, logo from the Meme Booth, ready-made X posts and a free Fat List listing.
